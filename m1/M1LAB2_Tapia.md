# Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The robot's gripper starts empty.
- The bread, peanut butter, and a knife are on the counter.

## Steps
- remove twist tie
-  Open the bag
- grab bread
- put down bread
- grab knife
- cut loaf into 2 slices
- put down knife
- grab slice 1
- put slice 1 on plate
- grab slice 2
- put down slice 2
- Open the peanut butter.
- remove seal
- Pick up the knife.
- scoop knife in jar
- Spread the peanut butter on slice.
- scoop knife in jar
- Spread the peanut butter on slice 2.
- put down knife
- close jar
- place loaf in bag
- close bread bag
- take slice 2
- place slice 2 onto slice


## Done When
The sandwich is finished.
